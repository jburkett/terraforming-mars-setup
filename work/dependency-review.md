# Dependency review — draft evidence, 2026-11-10

The user's preference for option B is tentative pending examples; no compatibility policy is approved or integrated into the specification yet. This is elaboration of clarification question 1, not an accepted answer or a new question.

## Verified dependencies and conditional behavior

- Prelude 2 requires Prelude. It does not require all of Venus Next/Colonies/Turmoil: cards specify individual dependencies and the rules allow prefiltering or discarding/redrawing incompatible physical cards. Source: https://fryxgames.se/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/TM_PRELUDE2_RULES_ENGi.pdf pp2–3.
- Extended Start is an optional variant in the Prelude 2 rules, changing the offered corporations/preludes to 3/6. Requiring Prelude 2 in the app for that variant is a proposed catalog policy, not a demonstrated physical necessity for those counts. The user has not approved this policy.
- Prelude, Venus Next, and Colonies each offer a variant dealing one corporation from that expansion and one from the others. Each such variant requires access to that corporation pool. Prelude explicitly permits using its corporations/projects without Prelude cards, so expansion ownership/content pool and enabled mechanics are distinct. Sources: Prelude p2, Venus p2, Colonies p2.
- Venus Next explicitly works with or without Corporate Era or any other expansion. It adds Hoverlord and Venuphile; World Government Terraforming is part of Venus rules, with an optional longer-game omission. Colonies repeats the Venus condition; Colonies alone does not introduce World Government Terraforming. Source: https://fryxgames.se/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/TM_VENUS_ENG_RULESi.pdf pp2–3; Colonies p3.
- Milestones & Awards chooses five replacement milestones and awards before dealing and retains Venus additions when Venus is enabled. Geologist must be replaced on maps without volcanic areas (Utopia has none). Generalist threshold changes without Corporate Era. Publisher page: https://fryxgames.se/product/terraforming-mars-milestones-awards/ . Download resolved to Swedish sheet: https://fryxgames.se/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/TM_MilestonesAndAwards_RULES_SEi.pdf . Its introduction permits swapping similar or expansion-specific entries rather than requiring enabling entire expansions.
- Inferred component dependencies from their conditions (not universal expansion prerequisites): Lobbyist and Politician need Turmoil; Pioneer needs Colonies; Planetologist needs Venus tags from Venus content. Constructor counts cities as well as colonies and therefore is not wholly unusable without Colonies. Trader counts resource types on cards, not colony trades, and is not a Colonies prerequisite. Printed versions and standalone tile versions have distinct thresholds; do not conflate them.
- Turmoil on Tharsis replaces the printed Terraformer milestone with its TR26 version. Applying this to every Terraformer-named tile would be unsupported: M&A's tile uses TR29. Source: https://fryxgames.se/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/TM_TURMOIL_ENG_RULESi.pdf p2, M&A publisher page.
- Amazonis/Vastitas delegate bonuses are ignored without Turmoil; these maps do not require enabling Turmoil. Amazonis changes parameters and component supply and offers an optional extended Venus board. Source: https://fryxgames.se/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/TM_AV_WRAP_ENG.pdf . The sheet alone does not establish a rule making Venus compulsory or fully specify the optional board's availability conditions; do not silently enforce Amazonis-only usage without more evidence.
- Terra Cimmeria's colony placement bonus is ignored without Colonies; Cimmeria does not require Colonies. Utopia's printed Pioneer depends on colonies and Cimmeria's printed Planetologist depends on Venus tags; disabled expansions can leave printed milestones unattainable, but that does not establish a whole-map expansion prerequisite. Source: https://fryxgames.se/product/terraforming-mars-utopia-cimmeria/ . Replacement/retention behavior for these printed entries needs resolution if rule evidence does not settle it.
- Colonies tile count depends on player count: 2→5, 3→5, 4→6, 5→7. Titan/Enceladus/Miranda remain in the draw pool but start inactive until a card can collect the respective resource. Do not mistake conditional activation for grounds to exclude the tile during setup. Source: https://cdn.1j1ju.com/medias/55/6a/42-terraforming-mars-colonies-rulebook.pdf pp2–3.
- Corporate Era changes deck composition and initial extra production. Base research drafting does not apply to initial setup dealing. Beginner Corporation changes dealing/initial purchase handling. Source: https://world-of-board-games.com.sg/docs/Terraforming-Mars.pdf pp7,13.

## Proposed pattern for human review
Auto-enable only genuine prerequisites of deliberately selected expansions/variants, explaining the change. Derive mandatory setup consequences automatically. For mixed-content decks and random public components, filter/instruct according to enabled content rather than automatically adding entire expansions. This pattern remains a proposal, including variant availability rules.
